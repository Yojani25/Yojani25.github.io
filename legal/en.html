5. How matches work

Worth knowing before you sit at a table:

• BOTS. If there are not enough people, the game fills the seats with automatic players. This happens at paid tables too: you can pay an entry fee and play against bots. Bots follow the same rules and cannot see anyone's tiles.

• LEAVING. If you leave, the game waits 30 seconds; then a bot takes your seat and you have 15 more seconds to come back. That time keeps running even if you close the app. If you do not, you lose the seat, the match continues without you, and it counts as a forfeit. The entry fee is not refunded.

• THE SERVER DECIDES. Entries, prizes, coins and results are calculated and stored by our server. If your phone disagrees with the server, the server is what counts.

3. Voice chat

Voice chat in private matches is direct between phones. We do not record, store or listen to that audio.

To let two phones find each other, public connection servers (STUN) operated by Google are used. On a direct connection, your IP address can be visible to others at the table. If you would rather avoid that, do not turn on the microphone.

The microphone only turns on when you enable it, and you can turn it off whenever you want.

Face ID or Touch ID lock: your phone does it. The app only receives a yes or a no; it never sees or stores your face, fingerprint or passcode. Turn it off in Settings.

Anyone at the table could record the conversation themselves with another device. That is outside our control.

4. Error reports

When the app crashes we send a technical report to Sentry: the error message, the line of code, the app version, your phone model and operating system, and your last steps in the game.

That report does NOT include your name, email, player name, or the text of any message. Personal-data sending is off and the user identifier is stripped before sending.

5. When you report a problem

The report button sends, along with what you write, a small technical bundle. This is exactly what is inside:

• the report category and app version;

• your phone model and browser, and your screen size;

• your language;

• a summary of your progress: coins, experience, level and wins;

• the code of the last room you played in;

• the internal log of your last steps.

It does not include your email, password, or messages. The report is stored in our database (Supabase) and only we can read it.

Reporting or blocking a person (from the table or the room) stores who reported, whom, the chosen reason and the room code; we review it ourselves. Your block list is kept in your account (or on your phone when you play as a guest): someone you block is muted for you, leaves your friends and cannot invite you again.

In-game chat is a set of prepared phrases, not free text, and is not stored anywhere: it is sent straight to your table and then it is gone.
